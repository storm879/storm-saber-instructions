⚠️ Safety Warnings

  • This is a collectible prop, not a toy. Children under 14 must not use it on their own; all minors must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • Contains a built-in lithium-polymer (Li-Po) battery. Never disassemble it, puncture the cells, submerge the saber or place it near a heat source.
  • Charge only from a standard 5 V charger (1–2 A). Fast charging can burn out the circuit.
  • The PC blade is custom-made. Never strike it violently; damage caused by misuse is not covered by the warranty.
  • For long storage: charge fully, then enter deep sleep. Recharge every 2–3 months so the battery does not over-discharge and lock up.
  • Do not look directly into the LEDs or the emitter, and never point the blade at anyone’s eyes.
  • Keep clear of people, glass and fragile objects. Never strike faces or heads.
  • Do not block the ventilation holes, and avoid damp storage to prevent oxidation and short circuits on the mainboard.
  • Heat near the emitter is normal — the hilt helps dissipate it. Never use the saber without the hilt fitted.

🎮 Button Operation

Click = short press and release. Hold = press and keep holding. Hold actions are confirmed by the number of button-LED flashes; powering on and off is indicated by a sound prompt.

States and LED indicator

  • Off (deep sleep): button LED off — hold for 3 s to power on (plays the “power on” tone)
  • Standby: button LED on, blade off — click to ignite
  • Ignited: button LED on, blade lit — hold for 1.5 s to retract (LED flashes 3×)

Standby mode

  • Click: ignite the blade
  • Hold 0.5 s (LED flashes 1×): adjust the volume
  • Hold 1 s (LED flashes 2×): switch the background light effect
  • Hold 1.5 s (LED flashes 3×): switch the light-effect mode
  • Hold 2 s (LED flashes 4×): switch to the previous sound font
  • Rotate the blade tip downward (counter-clockwise): switch to the next sound font
  • Rotate the blade tip upward: play background music

Ignited mode (blade on)

  • Strike: clash effect
  • Click: blaster-block effect
  • Hold 1 s (LED flashes 2×): continuous color sweep
  • Point the blade down, hold the button and rotate: jump color change / rotating color change
  • Click during color selection: confirm the current color
  • Hold the button and strike: blade lock-up
  • Touch the blade tip to the ground: drag effect
  • Touch the blade tip to a wall: melt effect
  • Point the blade up, hold the button and rotate: Force effect / Force-lightning block
  • Thrust forward: stab effect
  • Hold 1.5 s (LED flashes 3×): retract the blade
  • Hold 0.5 s (LED flashes 1×): enter / exit battle mode
  • Hold the button and swing: enter / exit continuous block mode

Features that must be enabled in the app

  • Battle mode
  • Continuous block mode
  • Force-lightning block

Note: motion sensitivity is adjustable in Xeno Configurator. Lower sensitivity is recommended for dueling to avoid false triggers.

💾 SD Card & Sound Font Guide

Sound font folder rules

  • Each sound font goes in its own folder.
  • Folder names may contain only A–Z, 0–9 and underscores — no Chinese characters, spaces or special symbols.
  • Required audio files: boot.wav, hum.wav, clash.wav, swing.wav, stab.wav, blaster.wav, lockup.wav, poweron.wav, poweroff.wav.
  • Audio format: WAV, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, mono.

SD card steps

  • Format the card as FAT32 with 4096-byte allocation units.
  • Put the font folders in the root directory — nested folders are not supported.
  • Do not modify the system files on the card; only add sound font folders.
  • Eject the card safely, reinsert it, then hold for 2 s in standby mode to switch the sound font.
  • Recommended capacity: 4–16 GB; 32 GB is the largest stable size.

📱 App

The app is used to connect to the saber, set colors, manage sound fonts and background music, and update the firmware.

Download and connect

  • Install Xeno Configurator from your phone’s app store (in China the app is called XENO SABER APP).
  • Power the saber on (standby), then enable Bluetooth on your phone.
  • In the app, tap the blue arrow to search for the saber, then confirm the prompt.
  • When the connection succeeds, the app shows a notice and the blade plays a confirmation sound.
  • If it will not connect, check the phone’s Bluetooth and Location permissions, move closer to the saber and try again.
  • One app can recognize and manage several sabers.

Main screen

  • Saber name / ID and an estimated battery level (it fluctuates; for reference only)
  • Base light-effect buttons: tap to switch
  • Three ways to select a color
  • Kyber crystal: tap to ignite or turn off; while the crystal is off, the saber stays in standby
  • Detailed light-effect settings (gradient, flicker, etc.)
  • Background music switch
  • Sound font (FONT) selection
  • Volume: 0–100, continuous adjustment
  • Bottom tabs: Home · Settings · Custom · My Saber

Firmware and permissions

  • Firmware updates are performed in the app. Keep the connection alive and do not cut the power during an update.
  • Bluetooth permission is required; on Android, Location permission is also needed to scan for Bluetooth devices. Storage permission is used to import / export profiles, and network permission is used for updates.

🔋 Charging, Contact Care & Optional Accessories

Charging

  • Red LED = charging; green LED = fully charged.
  • A full charge takes about 2–3 hours. Unplug once full and avoid leaving the saber on overnight trickle charge.
  • Quick-swap design: you can replace the 16650 lithium battery yourself. Replace it promptly if it swells or loses capacity.
  • After a deep discharge or a long period in storage, the battery may enter sleep mode: charge for at least 10 minutes to wake it. If it still does not charge, try another battery.
  • The estimated battery level is shown on the app’s main screen (for reference only).

Blade contact care

  • The gold contacts at the base of the blade and the spring pins in the hilt carry the power; dust and oxidation cause flickering or no light.
  • Clean them with a lint-free swab and anhydrous alcohol, and let them dry completely before installing the blade.
  • Push the blade all the way in, then gently tighten the set screws; never force them, or the socket can be damaged.

Installing and removing the blade

  • Switch the saber off before installing or removing the blade.
  • Push the blade fully into the emitter so the contacts seat, then gently tighten the set screws to secure it.
  • To remove: loosen the set screws, pull the blade straight out and store it in a dry place.
  • Check the contacts after every blade change — see “Blade contact care” above.

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • Will not power on after charging: the battery may be in sleep mode after a deep discharge or long storage — charge for at least 10 minutes to wake it. If it still does not respond, try another battery and contact support.
  • No swing, hum or clash sounds: charge the battery fully first, then check that the sound files are on the SD card and that the card is seated properly.
  • The saber suddenly stops working: low battery, or the SD card is loose or its files are corrupted — charge fully and reinsert the card.
  • The area around the emitter gets very hot: this is normal during use; always use the saber with the hilt fitted so it can dissipate heat — never use it without the hilt.
  • The blade is dark or flickers: power off, remove the blade, clean the blade contacts and the spring pins in the hilt, refit the blade and tighten the set screws.
  • The charging indicator does not light up: replace the charger.
  • A newly added sound font does not appear: check the folder name, FAT32 formatting, nested folders and the audio specification.
  • Motion triggers too easily or not at all: adjust the sensitivity in the app.
  • A continuous warning tone at power-on: the SD card files are corrupted — format the card and restore the factory firmware pack.

📜 Care & Warranty

Care tips

  • Wipe the hilt with a soft, slightly damp cloth; do not use sandpaper or corrosive cleaners.
  • Do not spray WD-40 or large amounts of alcohol inside the hilt — it corrodes the circuit board.
  • For long-term storage: clean the contacts, charge fully, enter deep sleep and keep the saber in a dry place away from direct light.
  • Opening the hilt yourself voids the warranty immediately.

Warranty terms

  • 12-month mainboard warranty from the date of delivery.
  • Covered: factory soldering defects and original hardware faults on the mainboard.
  • Not covered: blade wear (consumable), drop damage, water damage, unauthorized teardown, damage from hot-plugging the blade, and battery damage from fast charging.
  • After-sales process: contact support with a video of the fault; once assessed, return the saber to the workshop for repair.

🎮 按键操作说明

Click = 短按松开；Hold = 长按不放。长按操作以按钮 LED 闪烁次数确认；开关机为提示音。

状态与指示灯

  • 关机（深度休眠）：按钮灯灭 —— 长按 3 秒开机，有 power on 提示音
  • 待机：按钮灯亮、剑刃熄灭 —— 单击出刃
  • 开机：按钮灯亮、剑刃发光 —— 长按 1.5 秒收刃（LED 闪 3 次）

待机状态

  • 单击按钮：打开剑刃
  • 长按 0.5 秒（LED 闪 1 次）：音量调节
  • 长按 1 秒（LED 闪 2 次）：切换背景光效
  • 长按 1.5 秒（LED 闪 3 次）：切换光效模式
  • 长按 2 秒（LED 闪 4 次）：切换上一套音效包
  • 剑刃向下逆时针旋转：切换下一套音效包
  • 剑刃向上旋转：背景音乐

开机状态（剑刃点亮）

  • 敲击：敲击效果
  • 单击按钮：格挡爆能枪效果（打枪效果）
  • 长按 1 秒（LED 闪 2 次）：无极变色
  • 剑刃向下 + 按住按钮 + 旋转：跳动变色 / 旋转变色
  • 变色过程中单击按钮：选中当前颜色
  • 按住按钮 + 敲击：拼剑效果
  • 剑刃尖端向下戳地面：拖剑效果
  • 剑刃尖端戳墙：融化效果
  • 剑刃向上 + 按住按钮 + 旋转：原力效果 / 原力闪电格挡
  • 往前戳：刺效果
  • 长按 1.5 秒（LED 闪 3 次）：收刃
  • 长按 0.5 秒（LED 闪 1 次）：进入 / 退出战斗模式
  • 按住按钮 + 挥舞：进入 / 退出连续格挡模式

需在 App 内开启的功能

  • 战斗模式
  • 连续格挡模式
  • 原力闪电格挡

备注：体感灵敏度在 App 内可调；对打玩家建议调低灵敏度，防止误触发。

💾 SD 卡与音效包 Font 教程

音效包文件夹规范

  • 每个音效包使用独立文件夹。
  • 文件夹命名只允许 A–Z、0–9 和下划线；禁止中文、空格和特殊符号。
  • 必需音频文件：boot.wav, hum.wav, clash.wav, swing.wav, stab.wav, blaster.wav, lockup.wav, poweron.wav, poweroff.wav。
  • 音频规格：WAV，16 bit，44100 Hz，单声道。

SD 卡操作步骤

  • 格式化为 FAT32，分配单元大小 4096 字节。
  • Font 文件夹直接放在根目录，禁止多层嵌套。
  • 不要修改主板系统文件，只新增 Font 文件夹。
  • 安全弹出 SD 卡后插回光剑，在待机状态下长按 2 s 切换音效包。
  • 推荐容量 4–16 GB；最大稳定容量 32 GB。

🔋 充电、触点维护、选配配件

充电说明

  • 红灯 = 充电中；绿灯 = 充满。
  • 充满约 2–3 小时，充满后建议拔线，不要长期浮充过夜。
  • 快速换电设计，用户可自行更换16650型号锂电池；电池鼓包 / 衰减请及时更换
  • 电量耗尽或长期存放后，电池可能进入休眠：先充电 10 分钟以上唤醒；仍充不进电时，换一个电池。
  • 剩余电量可在 App 主界面查看（预估值，仅供参考）。

刃体触点维护

  • 刃体底部镀金触点、柄内弹片是导电核心，灰尘氧化会造成闪灯、无光。
  • 清理：无尘棉签 + 无水酒精擦拭，完全晾干再装刃体。
  • 刃体推到底，轻柔拧紧顶丝，禁止用力拧死压坏底座。

刃体安装与拆卸

  • 安装或拆卸刃体前，先关闭光剑电源。
  • 将刃体完全推入刃座，使触点接触到位，再轻拧顶丝固定。
  • 拆卸：松开顶丝，垂直拔出刃体，存放于干燥处。
  • 每次更换刃体后检查触点状态，参见上方「刃体触点维护」。

🔧 故障排查

  • 充电后无法开机：电量耗尽或长期存放后电池可能进入休眠，先充电 10 分钟以上唤醒；仍无反应时换一个电池测试，并联系客服。
  • 没有挥剑声、嗡鸣声或碰撞声：先把电充满，再检查音效文件是否在 SD 卡上、SD 卡是否插到位。
  • 光剑突然停止工作：电量不足，或 SD 卡松动 / 文件损坏 —— 充满电并重新插卡。
  • 发射器附近很烫：使用中发热属正常，必须装好柄体帮助散热；不要在没有装柄体的情况下使用。
  • 刃体不亮或闪烁：关机取出刃体，清洁刃体触点与柄内弹片，重新装刃并拧紧顶丝。
  • 充电指示灯不亮：更换充电器
  • 找不到新导入的音效包：检查文件夹命名、FAT32 格式、是否有嵌套、音频规格是否正确。
  • 感应过度灵敏或没反应：在 App 内调整灵敏度；
  • 开机持续警报音：SD 卡文件损坏 —— 格式化 SD 卡，重装出厂固件包。

📜 保养与质保条款

保养提示

  • 柄体脏污用微湿软布擦拭，禁止砂纸、腐蚀性清洁剂。
  • 不要向柄内部喷 WD-40 或大量酒精，会腐蚀电路板。
  • 长期收纳：清洁触点、充满电、深度休眠，存放于干燥避光处。
  • 私自拆开柄壳直接失去质保。

质保说明

  • 签收起 12 个月主板质保。
  • 质保覆盖主板出厂焊接、原生硬件故障。
  • 不保：刃体耗材、摔落磕碰、进水、私自拆机、带电插拔烧毁、快充损坏电池。
  • 售后流程：联系客服并提供故障视频，评估后返厂维修。
